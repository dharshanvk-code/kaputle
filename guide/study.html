Running a fair test

  1. Use the same piece of furniture for everyone, and take it apart again between sessions.
  2. Alternate: first person paper, second person 3D, and so on, so both groups are mixed.
  3. Don’t help unless they’re truly stuck. If you do, tap Got stuck for them.
  4. Count mistakes as you watch: a part fitted wrong, upside down or in the wrong place, or a step they had to undo.
  5. At the end, hand them the device for the questions, then fill in the facilitator part yourself.
